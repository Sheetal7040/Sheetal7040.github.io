01 / ABOUT

Curiosity on a cosmic scale.

I'm interested in connecting theoretical astrophysics with computational methods to investigate the structure and evolution of our universe.

My academic background includes astronomy, cosmology, radio interferometry, and numerical methods. During my master's thesis at IIT Indore, I worked on baseline design for a radio interferometer, developing a Python pipeline for simulation and baseline optimisation.

I'm particularly interested in dark matter, large-scale structure, gravitational lensing, and cosmological simulations — and in developing computational tools that help turn scientific questions into measurable results.

PROJECT 01 ◉
U–V PLANE / BASELINE COVERAGE

MASTER'S THESIS · RADIO ASTRONOMY

Radio Interferometer Baseline Design

Developed a Python pipeline for interferometer simulation and baseline optimisation using Monte Carlo random sampling, with an emphasis on improving (u,v) coverage and reducing sidelobe levels. The work included validation against GMRT coverage and site analysis for IIT Indore.
